# Outreach Templates

Use these as human-reviewed starting points. Replace bracketed text only with verified information.

## Cold Call Opener

Hi [name], this is [rep] with Autovyne. I noticed [verified observation]. We help local [industry] businesses respond to missed calls and web leads faster. I made a quick revenue-impact simulator for businesses like yours. Would it be unreasonable to take 30 seconds and see whether it is relevant?

## Discovery Questions

- What usually happens when a customer calls while everyone is busy or after hours?
- Roughly how many new inquiries come in during a typical month?
- How quickly does someone follow up on a missed call or web form?
- Which part of booking and follow-up takes the most staff time?

## Voicemail

Hi [name], this is [rep] with Autovyne. I put together a quick missed-call revenue simulator for local [industry] businesses. I’ll send the link so you can test it with your own numbers. If it looks useful, I’d be glad to walk through it with you.

## First Email

Subject: quick missed-call check for [business name]

I built a short simulator you can test with your own numbers: [demo link]

If it shows a meaningful gap, I can map out a simple first automation for [business name].

[rep]

## Follow-Up

Hi [name],

Following up with one practical question: when a new call is missed today, how quickly does someone usually respond?

That answer is normally enough to tell whether an automated response and booking flow would be useful. Here is the simulator again: [demo link]

[rep]

## Stop Conditions

Do not send or call when the lead opted out, is on a suppression list, has unclear contact eligibility, or has already received a pending outreach action.
