# Autovyne Legal and Compliance Template Library

This is an internal drafting library for Autovyne. It is not legal advice and does not replace review by a licensed attorney. Use it to keep public pages, onboarding scripts, customer agreements, outreach scripts, and automation prompts consistent.

## Official Source Map

Source links last reviewed: June 13, 2026.

- FCC TCPA / robocall and robotext rules: 47 CFR 64.1200 and FCC orders on consent revocation and AI-generated voices. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/part-64/section-64.1200
- FCC AI voice ruling: AI-generated voices can fall within TCPA artificial/prerecorded voice restrictions. https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal
- FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule: 16 CFR Part 310; disclosures, misrepresentation limits, call-time limits, do-not-call handling, and payment restrictions. https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/telemarketing-sales-rule
- FTC CAN-SPAM business guidance and CAN-SPAM Rule: 16 CFR Part 316; accurate headers/subject lines, ad disclosure where applicable, physical address, and opt-out handling. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- FTC Negative Option / recurring subscription guidance: disclose billing terms clearly, obtain affirmative authorization, keep proof, and make cancellation practical. The FTC's 2024 click-to-cancel amendment was vacated by the Eighth Circuit in 2025, so treat it as a consumer-friendly benchmark and watch item rather than a settled active federal rule. https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/negative-option-rule
- Eighth Circuit watch item: Custom Communications, Inc. v. FTC, No. 24-3137, vacating the 2024 Negative Option Rule amendments on procedural grounds. https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/25/07/243137P.pdf
- FTC AI claims enforcement guidance: no "AI exemption" from deceptive/unfair-practice laws; avoid exaggerated claims, unsupported performance promises, and deceptive AI disclosures. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check
- Missouri telemarketing/no-call rules: Missouri Revised Statutes Chapter 407 and Missouri Attorney General No-Call guidance. https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=407.1098
- Federal call-recording baseline: 18 USC 2511; state law may require stricter all-party consent. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2511&edition=prelim
- CCPA/CPRA-style privacy rights: California Attorney General and CPPA guidance for notice, access, deletion, correction, opt-out, and retention concepts. https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa

## Core Drafting Rules

1. Do not promise guaranteed revenue, guaranteed compliance, or guaranteed results.
2. Say "estimated," "may," "can help," and "requires setup/review" when discussing ROI or automation.
3. SMS and AI calling must be consent-aware and opt-out-aware.
4. Any call recording/transcription workflow should disclose recording/AI processing and account for state-specific consent rules.
5. Use "human review required" for compliance-sensitive actions such as opt-out reversals, caller blocking, privacy requests, and billing changes.
6. Keep legal entity name, business address, cancellation instructions, and refund rules current in every public policy.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. View our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

## SMS No-Sharing Template

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

## STOP/HELP Template

Recipients can opt out by replying STOP. Recipients can request help by replying HELP or contacting Autovyne at kwaun.autovyne@gmail.com. Opt-out requests should be honored promptly and follow-up must remain paused unless a lawful basis and updated consent record exists.

## AI Call and Recording Disclosure Template

Autovyne workflows may use AI to answer, summarize, transcribe, classify, route, or analyze calls and messages. Calls and messages may be recorded or transcribed for service delivery, quality control, compliance, and customer-support purposes. Customers are responsible for giving any required notices and obtaining any required consent from their callers, leads, customers, staff, and users before enabling recording, transcription, AI voice, or automated follow-up.

## Customer Lead Consent Responsibility Template

The customer is responsible for ensuring that its own leads and customers have provided any notices, permissions, and consents required for call recording, AI-assisted communication, SMS follow-up, email follow-up, CRM processing, and automated workflows. Autovyne may refuse, pause, or require review of workflows when consent, suppression, or legal basis is unclear.

## Do-Not-Contact Template

If a person asks not to be contacted, uses an opt-out keyword, disputes consent, says the number is wrong, or otherwise clearly indicates they do not want further outreach, Autovyne and the customer must treat that request as a do-not-contact request until reviewed. Future outreach should stop unless the request is resolved and a lawful basis is documented.

If the recipient asks not to be contacted, end the call politely and mark Do Not Contact.

## Subscription and Cancellation Template

Autovyne subscriptions bill monthly unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Automatic billing is handled through Stripe. The first month is intended to cover onboarding, configuration, AI setup, workflow preparation, and launch support. Autovyne should disclose plan price, billing interval, renewal timing, cancellation path, and refund limitations before payment. After the first month, customers may cancel before the next billing period using the cancellation method stated in their order form, billing portal, or written customer agreement. Autovyne does not provide legal, financial, medical, or emergency services.

## Recurring Billing Operations Template

Before charging a recurring subscription, Autovyne should keep a record of the plan selected, amount, billing interval, customer identity, checkout/session ID, consent to recurring billing, billing method, terms accepted, and cancellation instructions shown. Cancellation and billing-help requests should be easy to find in the portal, routed to the admin queue, and handled promptly. Do not hide cancellation behind unrelated sales steps, unresolved onboarding tasks, or technical barriers.

## AI Claims Guardrail Template

Autovyne uses AI to assist with lead capture, missed-call follow-up, workflow routing, summaries, and operational recommendations. AI output may be incomplete or incorrect and should be reviewed by a human before relying on it for business decisions, customer communication, billing decisions, legal decisions, medical decisions, or emergency handling.

## ROI Disclaimer Template

ROI calculators and recovered-revenue metrics are estimates based on user-provided inputs, assumptions, observed activity, or customer-provided deal values. They are for planning and reporting only and are not promises, guarantees, or financial advice.

## Privacy Request Template

Customers and users may request access, correction, deletion review, do-not-contact review, or other privacy/data-handling help by contacting Autovyne. Autovyne may need to verify identity, authority, account ownership, legal requirements, billing status, and operational impact before acting on a request.

## Internal Approval Template

This request touches consent, privacy, billing, AI output, call recording, customer data, or communication preferences. Do not automate the requested change until an Autovyne admin reviews the record, verifies supporting evidence, and records the decision.
