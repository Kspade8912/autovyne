# Autovyne Sales Playbook

Use this for manual cold calls and demos. Keep it simple. The goal of a first call is not to explain every AI system; the goal is to find whether missed calls, slow follow-up, or lead chaos is painful enough for the owner to view the demo.

## Simple Sales Flow

1. Open with the missed-call problem, not the product.
2. Ask one qualifying question about missed calls or slow follow-up.
3. Offer the ROI + live demo as the next step.
4. If interested, send the signup link and explain payment -> onboarding -> portal activation.
5. After signup, use the onboarding checklist and customer portal to keep the owner informed.

## Offer Summary

Autovyne is a monthly managed AI automation service for local businesses. It helps recover missed-call and slow-follow-up revenue using AI calling, consent-aware SMS, CRM sync, workflow automation, and a simple client portal.

The customer is not buying fifteen separate apps. They are buying a managed workflow and a portal where they can see status, activity, controls, recovered-value estimates, support questions, and onboarding progress.

## What Happens After Payment

1. Stripe confirms the subscription or manual billing is flagged for owner review.
2. Autovyne activates the client portal.
3. Autovyne reviews business profile details, service rules, hours, FAQs, routing, and escalation contact.
4. Autovyne connects CRM, AI calling, SMS follow-up, n8n workflow handoff, and support paths in stages.
5. Customer-visible updates are added to the portal.
6. Customer approves scripts/workflow direction before live monitored usage.
7. Autovyne starts launch monitoring.

## What Not To Promise

- Do not guarantee revenue.
- Do not guarantee bookings.
- Do not guarantee legal compliance.
- Do not say AI is perfect or fully autonomous.
- Do not say SMS can be sent to anyone.
- Do not say call recording or AI voice can be enabled without customer/caller notice review.

Use phrases like:

- "estimate"
- "can help"
- "depending on your call volume"
- "after setup and review"
- "where consent allows"
- "we will test it before live use"

## Qualification Questions

- What usually happens when a customer calls while everyone is busy?
- How fast does someone follow up on missed calls?
- Do you know how many leads go unanswered in a normal month?
- Are you using a CRM now, or is follow-up mostly manual?
- Do you already text leads, and do you track consent?
- If a customer books, how do you make sure follow-up stops?
- Would seeing call/follow-up activity in one simple portal help you?

## Objection Handling

**"We already answer our phones."**

That is good. The question is what happens during peak hours, after hours, lunch breaks, weekends, and when the team is already with a customer.

**"We already have software."**

Autovyne can work as the simpler owner-facing layer. The point is not to replace every app; it is to make calls, follow-up, CRM, and workflow status easier to manage.

**"AI sounds risky."**

That is exactly why Autovyne uses review steps, portal controls, legal audit flags, and customer-visible updates. AI helps with speed, but sensitive actions still need review.

**"It costs too much."**

That may be true if missed-call volume is low. The demo helps estimate whether the possible lost revenue is meaningfully higher than the monthly cost.

**"Send me information."**

Absolutely. I will send the demo first because it is easier to judge with your own numbers than from a generic flyer.

## Scripts And Pitches

### 15-Second Opener

Hi, this is Kwaun with Autovyne. Quick question: when your team misses a call or gets busy, how fast does someone follow up with that lead?

### 30-Second Pitch

Autovyne helps local businesses catch missed calls, follow up faster, and keep leads organized without making the owner manage a bunch of apps. We use AI calling, consent-aware SMS, CRM updates, and a simple portal so you can see what is happening.

### Demo Close

I can show you a quick live demo that estimates what missed calls may be costing and what an automated follow-up workflow could look like. If it does not look useful, no pressure.

### Voicemail

Hi, this is Kwaun with Autovyne. I was calling about missed-call follow-up for your business. I have a quick demo that shows how much revenue can slip through when calls are missed or follow-up is slow. I will send the link if I have your email, or you can visit the Autovyne demo page.

### Not Interested Response

No problem. Before I let you go, is missed-call follow-up already handled well on your end, or is it just not a priority right now?

### Price Objection

That makes sense. The reason I start with the demo is to see whether the missed-call gap is larger than the monthly cost. If the numbers do not support it, I would not push you into it.
